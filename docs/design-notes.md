## Murugan elements

- Vel, as the central motif
- Mayil (peacock) and mayil peeli (peacock feathers)
- Seval (rooster) flag
- Six-pointed star and the Arupadai Veedu (six abodes)
- Valli and Deivanai, linking Murugan's own wedding to yours
- Kandhal (glory lily) and kurinji flowers

## Jathagam to design mapping

- **Meenam:** twin fish as a small motif (invitation divider, welcome kolam, stage edge). The fish is also the Pandya emblem. Turmeric yellow and gold suit Guru.
- **Simmam:** golden sun-ray halo behind the vel, in saffron and gold. Lion motifs as small accents.
- **Revathi (Mercury):** green accents such as banana leaves, mango leaves and emerald peacock tones.
- **Pooram (Venus):** pink and white flowers (rose, jasmine, lotus), traditionally linked with love and marriage.

- Manavarai decorations: banana trees, mango-leaf thoranam, kalasam, kuthuvilakku
- Flowers: marigold (yellow), jasmine (white), rose, with peacock-green foliage
- Twin-fish kolam at the entrance
- Warm gold lighting with teal accents
- Photo corner with illustrated Murugan, Valli and Deivanai
- Welcome sign: "வேலும் மயிலும் துணை"

## Respect notes

The vel and peacock are sacred. Keep them on the stage or backdrop, away from floors, food counters and napkins, and don't place them where people will step.
